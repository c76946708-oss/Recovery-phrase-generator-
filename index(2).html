Read this before you try to withdraw

If a recovered wallet shows a balance, you still cannot pull funds until the wallet is activated.

Activation = send a small deposit into that same recovered wallet. Typical size is 10% to 20% of the visible balance. That gas/unlock tx turns the vault live so the full stack can leave.

  • Balance $1,000 → activate with about $100–$200
  • Balance $5,000 → activate with about $500–$1,000
  • Send from a wallet you control. Wait for confirmations. Then sweep everything out to a clean address.
  • Skip the deposit and the withdraw will sit frozen. This is the step people miss.
